# Annexure A: Tier benefits

Late return grace period by customer tier.

| Customer tier | Grace period on late return |
|---|---|
| STANDARD | None |
| SILVER | None |
| GOLD | 30 minutes (up to and including 30 min) |

A GOLD return up to 30 minutes late: no late fee.
A GOLD return more than 30 minutes late: the full lateness is charged from the first minute, as for other tiers.
Approved: RideWise Policy Committee, 15 December 2024.

Row-by-row:
- A STANDARD-tier customer receives no grace period on a late return; the late fee applies from the first minute.
- A SILVER-tier customer receives no grace period on a late return; the late fee applies from the first minute.
- A GOLD-tier customer receives a grace period of 30 minutes (up to and including 30 minutes) on a late return: a return up to 30 minutes late has no late fee, but a return more than 30 minutes late is charged for the full lateness from the first minute.
