# Annexure 1: Refund schedule (Version 2)

Applies to bookings made on or after 1 July 2026, 00:00 IST.

| Cancellation before scheduled pickup | Refund of rental charge |
|---|---|
| 48 hours or more | 100% |
| 12 hours or more, but less than 48 hours | 75% |
| 2 hours or more, but less than 12 hours | 25% |
| Less than 2 hours | No refund (0%) |

Cancelled by RideWise: 100% refund in all cases.
No-show: no refund.
Approved: RideWise Policy Committee, 20 June 2026.

Row-by-row:
- If a Version 2 booking is cancelled 48 hours or more before the scheduled pickup, the refund of the rental charge is 100%.
- If a Version 2 booking is cancelled 12 hours or more but less than 48 hours before the scheduled pickup, the refund of the rental charge is 75%.
- If a Version 2 booking is cancelled 2 hours or more but less than 12 hours before the scheduled pickup, the refund of the rental charge is 25%.
- If a Version 2 booking is cancelled less than 2 hours before the scheduled pickup, there is no refund (0%).
- A Version 2 booking cancelled by RideWise is refunded 100% in all cases.
- A no-show under Version 2 receives no refund.
